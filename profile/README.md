# CypherHunter

**Crypto intelligence with traceable evidence.**

CypherHunter is a crypto research and discovery platform focused on
projects, fundraising, organizations, people, and their lifecycle.

We structure crypto ecosystem data so researchers and analysts can
track not only what exists today, but also what changed — and why.

## What we track

- Crypto projects, organizations, and people
- Fundraising rounds and investor activity
- Project lifecycle and phase changes
- Historical events and supporting evidence
- Exchange listings and delistings

## Data & Research

CypherHunter focuses on structured, verifiable data.

Lifecycle changes and historical records are progressively linked to
supporting evidence, including official announcements and archived sources.
